CONCEPT · 02 · REFRAME THE ROLE
The central idea

HR makes
work work better.

For employees. For leaders. For the organization.

05 MIN · DISCUSSION

The true role of HR is to help the organization achieve its goals through its people, while creating a fair, healthy and effective place to work.

TODAY

Deliver reliably

Pay, records, hiring, policies and employee support all matter.

ALWAYS

Improve the system

Notice patterns, advise leaders and help prevent recurring problems.

CONCEPT · 03 · THE FOUR CONTRIBUTIONS
A simple way to see the whole role

Four ways HR creates value.

01 / RUN

Make the essentials work

Deliver accurate, timely and consistent HR services people can trust.

02 / SUPPORT

Help people thrive

Listen, guide, resolve issues and create a better employee experience.

03 / ENABLE

Equip leaders

Help managers make sound decisions and build capable teams.

04 / SHAPE

Move the business forward

Spot workforce needs, build capability and connect people priorities to business goals.

Reflection: Which contribution gets the most attention today? Which one needs more?

08 MIN · FRAMEWORK
CONCEPT · 04 · CONNECT WORK TO IMPACT
The question behind every task

From “done” to “what changed?”

Good HR work is visible in the decisions and experiences it improves.

RECRUITMENT

Filled a vacancy → built a stronger team

Look at quality, speed and the candidate and manager experience.

LEARNING

Delivered training → improved capability

Check what people can now do differently in their work.

ENGAGEMENT

Ran an event → strengthened connection

Listen for feedback and follow through on what matters.

06 MIN · EXAMPLES
CONCEPT · 05 · EMPLOYEE JOURNEY
Think beyond a single transaction

HR is present at the moments that matter.

01 / JOIN

A promising start

Recruit fairly. Set expectations. Welcome people well.

02 / GROW

Room to develop

Build skills, give feedback and open opportunities.

03 / CONTRIBUTE

Conditions to succeed

Support managers, performance, wellbeing and trust.

04 / MOVE ON

A respectful transition

Handle exits well and learn from why people leave.

Every interaction can either strengthen or weaken confidence in HR.

06 MIN · JOURNEY
CONCEPT · 06 · PARTNERSHIP IN PRACTICE
A practical shift

Bring the right question, not just the answer.

Strong partnership means understanding the need before choosing the HR response.

06 MIN · DISCUSSION
LISTENWhat is the real problem behind the request?
TESTWhat do the facts, people and context tell us?
ADVISEWhich options are fair, workable and aligned with business needs?
FOLLOW UPDid the action improve the outcome?

Be a trusted partner: empathetic with people, clear with leaders and consistent in your judgment.

CONCEPT · 07 · EXCHANGE A REALISTIC CASE
Your turn · small group discussion

What would HR do next?

THE SITUATION

A team is losing new hires within their first three months.

The manager asks HR to recruit replacements quickly. Exit comments mention unclear expectations and limited support during onboarding.

The vacancy is real. So is the pattern.
01What would you clarify with the manager and employees?
02What immediate support is needed?
03What should change in the hiring or onboarding process?
04How would you know it worked?
15 MIN · 3 MIN THINK · 7 MIN DISCUSS · 5 MIN SHARE
CONCEPT · 08 · CONNECT THE DOTS
What good HR looks like here

Address the need. Change the pattern.

IMMEDIATE

Keep the team supported

Agree on hiring needs, check capacity and give new joiners a clear point of contact.

ROOT CAUSE

Understand the experience

Compare expectations, manager support, onboarding steps and early exit themes.

EVIDENCE

Measure the difference

Track early turnover, new hire feedback and time to effective performance.

HR serves the urgent need while helping the manager prevent the next one.
