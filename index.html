WELCOME TO THE HR EXCHANGE

Learning Across HR.
Impact Across Clusters.

We are excited to launch The HR Exchange, a new learning journey designed to bring our Regional and Cluster HR teams closer together through practical knowledge sharing, real business cases and open exchange.

The purpose is simple: share what works, strengthen our HR capabilities and create greater consistency and impact across our clusters.
